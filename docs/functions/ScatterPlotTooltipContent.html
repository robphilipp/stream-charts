      Adds tooltip content as a table, whose rows are the x-values and the y-values, and whose columns depend on where the mouse is over the series (as the plot reports it, see ScatterTooltipMetadata -- the plot also highlights the same points):

      When the mouse is over a line segment, the points before and after the mouse, and the difference between them:

      series name
                 before     after         ∆
      x-label     x_tb      x_ta       x_ta - x_tb
      y-label     y_tb      y_ta       y_ta - y_tb

      When the mouse is directly over a data point, that point (in bold), and the points before and after it:

      series name
                 before     point     after
      x-label     x_b        x_p       x_a
      y-label     y_b        y_p       y_a

      A point that doesn't exist (e.g. before the series' first point) shows a dash.

      Below the table, the statistics of the series' points in the window of data the chart holds: their count, the mean and (sample) standard deviation of their values, and the mean time between consecutive points. And below that, a sparkline of the autocorrelation of their values, by lag (in points, from 0 on the left), drawn from -1 (bottom) to 1 (top): with a y-axis (ticks at -1, 0, and 1), and an x-axis along the (dashed) zero line, with ticks at round lags, captioned "lag" (just to the right of the axis' end).

      Built on TooltipContent; put it inside a Tooltip.
