    Interface GeoLayer

    Optional Readonlylabel

    label?: (feature: Feature) => string | undefined

    A feature's label (e.g. its name), drawn at its middle when the feature is wholly in view, and the label fits inside it as drawn (so that labels come and go with the zoom, rather than piling up); and a point's beside its dot, when it's in view, and doesn't overlap a label of the layer drawn before it (so put the most important points first). No labels when not set.
