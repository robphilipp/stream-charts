    Optionalshape

    shape?: "circles" | "pies" | "cells"

    How the aggregates are drawn:

    • circles (the default): a circle per cell, sized by sizeBy, and colored by colorBy;
    • pies: a circle per cell, sized by sizeBy, and divided into a wedge per group (see the roll-up's groupBy): each wedge's angle is the group's share of the cell's events, and its color the group's own colorBy value (or, without colorBy, the group's color, see groupColors) -- e.g. each sensor's mean delay, within each area. A cell without groups is drawn as a plain circle;
    • cells: the cells themselves (for a roll-up whose cells are areas, see byGridCell), colored by colorBy (or by sizeBy, without one). A roll-up whose cells aren't areas is drawn as circles.

    OptionalsizeDomain

    sizeDomain?: [number, number]

    The fixed [lowest, largest] range of the sizeBy values that the sizes span: an aggregate at (or below) the lowest value gets the smallest radius, and one at (or above) the largest gets the largest, with the circles' areas growing in proportion in between. Defaults to [0, the largest value shown], so the sizes are relative to the largest aggregate.
